# Licence of the voice recordings

The audio files in this folder (`*.ogg`) are speech made with the **"hfc_female" voice for Piper** and then raised in pitch.

- Voice: `en_US-hfc_female-medium`, from the Piper voices collection (https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices),
  trained on the HiFi-CAPTAIN corpus by Mike Ng.
- Text-to-speech program: Piper, by the Rhasspy / Open Home Foundation project.
